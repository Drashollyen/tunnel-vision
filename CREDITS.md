# Credits

Third-party assets used in Tunnel Vision. **When you add anything from the Asset Store (or anywhere else), add it here in the same PR.**

Keep store assets under `Assets/ThirdParty/<Asset Name>/` and don't edit their files. Copy anything you want to change into `Assets/_Project/`, so a store update can't overwrite your work.

## Note on Asset Store licensing

This repository is public. Asset Store items, including free ones, are licensed under the Asset Store EULA, which covers using them in your game, not redistributing the source files. The group chose to keep them in the public repository. If a publisher objects, remove that asset from the repo and its history, and have each member import it from their own Asset Store account instead.
